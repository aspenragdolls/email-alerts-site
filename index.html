email-summary-alerts

A private, personal email assistant.

email-summary-alerts is a personal tool used by its developer only. It reads the developer's own Gmail inbox with read-only access, uses an AI model running on the developer's own computer to rank new emails by urgency and importance, and sends short summaries of the important ones to the developer's phone through a private Telegram bot.

It is not offered to the public, and it cannot send, delete, or modify email.
